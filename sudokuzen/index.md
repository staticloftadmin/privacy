# SudokuZen Privacy Policy

SudokuZen is made by Static Loft. Effective date: 2026-11-05.

## Summary

SudokuZen has no accounts, no ads and no third-party tracking. By default it sends a small set of anonymous usage counts to Static Loft's own server so we can see how the app is used. They cannot be linked to you. You can turn this off at any time in Settings > Privacy.

## Anonymous usage statistics

**What is sent.**

- When the app is opened.
- For each game: its difficulty, time played, mistakes, hints used and how far you got (when a game is started, completed or abandoned).
- Your app version, major system version and platform (iOS or macOS).
- A device timestamp on each event.

**What is never sent.**

- No account, device or advertising identifiers.
- No name, email or location.
- No puzzle contents or photos.
- Your IP address is not stored.

**How it is kept.** The statistics are sent over HTTPS to Static Loft's own server (`stats-api.staticloft.com`). The app proves it is a genuine copy of SudokuZen with Apple's App Attest or a small proof-of-work. App Attest uses a key created for SudokuZen on your device; the server keeps only a hash of its id, apart from the statistics, and never links the two. Devices without App Attest send a random tag that changes every day, used to count daily users, and deleted each night. The connection is processed by our hosting provider, Cloudflare, which derives the country as a two-letter code. IP addresses are not stored. Individual events are deleted after 90 days, and only daily totals are kept.

**How to turn it off.** Open Settings > Privacy and turn off "Share anonymous statistics". Anything not yet sent is discarded. Settings > Privacy > What is shared lists the same information as this page.

## iCloud sync

Your games, history, lesson progress and settings are stored in your private iCloud storage (CloudKit and iCloud key-value storage), under your Apple Account. Static Loft cannot access them.

## Paper puzzle scanning

When you scan a paper puzzle, camera images and photos you choose are processed on your device and never leave it.

## No third-party SDKs, ads or tracking

SudokuZen contains no third-party SDKs, no advertising and no cross-app or cross-site tracking.

## Children

SudokuZen collects no data from anyone beyond what is described above, including from children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Changes to what the app sends will also be reflected in Settings > Privacy > What is shared.
