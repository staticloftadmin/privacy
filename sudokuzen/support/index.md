# SudokuZen Support

SudokuZen is a Sudoku game for iPhone, iPad and Mac, made by Static Loft. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, hints explain their reasoning, and short lessons teach the solving techniques.

## Contact

Questions, bug reports and ideas are welcome at [scott@staticloft.com](mailto:scott@staticloft.com). Please tell us your device, your iOS or macOS version and what happened. We read every message.

## Common questions

### My games do not sync between my devices

Sync uses iCloud, so every device must be signed in to the same Apple Account and have iCloud turned on. On iPhone and iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, and check that SudokuZen is allowed to use iCloud. On a Mac, open System Settings > Apple Account > iCloud. Changes can take a short while to arrive; opening the app on both devices speeds it up.

### How do I scan a paper puzzle?

Choose the scan option on the home screen and point the camera at the puzzle. For the best result, use good light, hold the phone parallel to the page so the whole grid is in view, keep the grid sharp and avoid glare or shadows across it. Printed grids work best. If a digit is read wrongly you can correct it before you start playing.

### How do I turn off anonymous statistics?

Open Settings > Privacy and turn off the sharing of anonymous usage statistics. They never contain an account, device or advertising identifier, and nothing else in the app depends on them. See the privacy policy below for exactly what is sent.

### I closed the app and my game is gone

It is not. SudokuZen saves your game as you play, including after a force quit, and offers to continue it from the home screen.

### How do I see how a puzzle was solved?

Finished games are kept in History, and each one can be replayed move by move.
